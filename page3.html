About Us

Cantina 76 is an independently owned and operated Mexican-inspired restaurant and bar with locations in Columbia, Charleston, Kiawah, and Greenville, South Carolina.

We specialize in creative tacos, hand-crafted margaritas, and an upbeat atmosphere that makes every visit enjoyable. Whether you are a University of South Carolina student, a local family, a working professional, or a visitor exploring Columbia, Cantina 76 offers a welcoming dining experience for everyone.

With approximately 100 employees across our locations, we are proud to be a growing mid-sized business that values great food, excellent service, and community connections. We are also pet-friendly, so feel free to bring your furry family members along!

The Origin Story

Cantina 76 began with a simple idea: create a place where people could enjoy bold Mexican-inspired flavors in a fun and welcoming environment. Three friends who shared a passion for food and hospitality noticed that many restaurants focused on either great food or great atmosphere, but rarely both.

Inspired by family recipes, local culture, and the vibrant energy of Columbia, they launched Cantina 76. Their goal was to bring together students, families, professionals, and visitors for fresh tacos, handcrafted margaritas, and unforgettable experiences. Today, that vision continues at every Cantina 76 location.
